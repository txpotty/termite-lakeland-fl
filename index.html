Liquid Termite Treatments

Creating a protective barrier around your home is a common and highly effective method. We apply liquid termiticides to the soil surrounding your foundation, creating a treated zone that termites cannot penetrate. As termites forage through the treated soil, they pick up the termiticide and carry it back to the colony, effectively eliminating it. This method offers immediate protection and long-term residual effects. The precise application of these products is crucial for maximum effectiveness and minimal environmental impact.

Termite Baiting Systems

Termites are attracted to the bait, which contains a slow-acting insecticide. The termites consume the bait and carry it back to their colony, where it is shared, leading to the gradual elimination of not just the foraging termites but the entire colony. This method can be particularly effective for monitoring and controlling termite activity over time. The placement and monitoring of these systems are critical for successful long-term control.

Smarter Solutions for Common Pest and Termite Problems in Lakeland

Understanding the common signs of a pest or termite infestation is critical for timely intervention and minimizing damage to your Lakeland home. Many pests can silently infiltrate your property, causing structural damage, posing health risks, or simply being a nuisance. Being aware of the telltale signs can help you act quickly and seek professional assistance before the problem escalates. Early detection is crucial for effective and less costly pest control.

Termite Infestation Signs

Termites, especially subterranean termites which are prevalent in Florida, are notorious for their ability to cause extensive damage undetected. They work silently, often within the walls or under floors, making their presence known only when the damage becomes significant. Recognizing the subtle signs of an infestation can save you thousands of dollars in repairs. Knowledge is your first line of defense against these destructive pests.

Mud Tubes

Subterranean termites build mud tubes, also known as ‘shelter tubes,’ to travel between their underground colonies and wooden structures in your home. These tubes are typically found on foundation walls, crawl spaces, or beams. They provide protection from predators and the environment, maintaining the high humidity termites require to survive. The presence of mud tubes is a strong indicator of termite activity and should be investigated immediately. Disturbing a mud tube and observing if it is quickly repaired or rebuilt is a common test for active infestation.

Discarded Wings

Termite swarmers, the reproductive members of a termite colony, emerge from the colony to begin new ones. After swarming, they shed their wings. Piles of discarded wings, often found near windowsills, doors, or light fixtures, can be a sign of a recent or active termite swarm. Swarming usually occurs during warm, humid weather, particularly after rain. These discarded wings are a definitive sign that termites are present on or near your property. Do not ignore this crucial warning sign.

Damaged Wood

Termites consume wood from the inside out, leaving the outer surface intact. This can make it difficult to detect damage initially. However, tapping on suspect wood with a screwdriver can reveal hollow-sounding sections. As the infestation progresses, you may notice sagging floors, buckling walls, or ceilings that appear water-damaged (as termites introduce moisture). Wood damage is often the most visible, but also the most concerning, sign of a termite problem. The extent of the damage can vary significantly depending on the duration and size of the infestation.

Frass (Termite Droppings)

Drywood termites, another type of termite found in Florida, do not build mud tubes. Instead, they create small kick-out holes in infested wood and push out their fecal pellets, known as ‘frass.’ These pellets are small, granular, and often the color of the wood the termites are consuming. Piles of frass that resemble sawdust near wooden structures can indicate a drywood termite infestation. Locating these small piles can help pinpoint the source of the drywood termite problem. Proper identification of the type of termite is crucial for effective treatment.

Tight-fitting Doors or Windows

Termite activity can cause wood to swell and distort, leading to doors and windows that are difficult to open or close. While this can sometimes be attributed to moisture, it can also be a symptom of a hidden termite infestation within the wooden frames. If you experience unexplained difficulty with doors or windows, it’s worth considering the possibility of termite activity. This subtle sign can often be overlooked but can point to a significant underlying issue.

Common Household Pest Problems in Lakeland

Beyond termites, Lakeland homes can be susceptible to a variety of other common pests, including ants, roaches, spiders, rodents, and more. While not always as structurally damaging as termites, these pests can still pose health risks, contaminate food, and create an uncomfortable living environment. Effective pest control services address these issues comprehensively.

Ant Infestations

Ants are one of the most common household pests, often entering homes in search of food and water. While most ants are relatively harmless, some species like fire ants can deliver painful stings, and carpenter ants can cause structural damage by tunneling through wood. Identifying the type of ant and their entry points is crucial for effective control. Ant infestations can quickly become overwhelming if not addressed promptly. Our services include targeted treatments for various ant species.

Cockroaches

Cockroaches are not only unsightly but also carry bacteria and allergens that can aggravate asthma and allergies. They are highly adaptable and can thrive in various environments. Finding even one cockroach can suggest a larger hidden population. Eliminating cockroach infestations requires a comprehensive approach that includes sanitation recommendations and targeted insecticide treatments. Their presence is a serious health concern that should not be ignored.

Spiders

While most spiders found in homes are harmless, some species, like the brown recluse or black widow, can pose a health risk with their bites. Spiders are often attracted to homes where there are other insect pests for them to feed on. Our pest control services can help reduce the insect population, making your home less attractive to spiders. We also offer targeted treatments for spider infestations. If you’re concerned about venomous spiders, professional intervention is highly recommended.

Rodents (Rats and Mice)

Rats and mice can cause significant damage to property by gnawing on wires, insulation, and structural elements. They also carry diseases and contaminate food sources. Their presence is a serious health and safety hazard. Rodent infestations require a multi-faceted approach that includes trapping, exclusion (sealing entry points), and sanitation recommendations. Addressing a rodent problem promptly is essential for preventing damage and health risks. Our comprehensive rodent control services provide effective solutions.

Signs You Need Pest Control Services

Recognizing the signs of a pest problem can help you seek professional help before the situation gets out of hand. Ignoring early indicators can lead to more extensive damage and costlier treatments down the line. Be observant and take action if you notice any of these signs.

Visible Pests

The most obvious sign of a pest problem is seeing live pests in your home. Whether it’s ants marching across your countertop, a cockroach scurrying across the floor, or a mouse in your pantry, visible pests indicate an active infestation. Even seeing a single pest can be a sign that there are many more hidden from view. Don’t assume it’s just a lone pest; investigate further or call a professional.

Pest Droppings or Fecal Matter

Pest droppings are a clear indicator of their presence. The size and shape of the droppings can help identify the type of pest. For example, mouse droppings are small and pellet-shaped, while rat droppings are larger. Finding droppings in pantries, under sinks, or in attics suggests an active infestation that needs to be addressed. Proper identification of droppings can help tailor the treatment strategy.

Gnaw Marks

Rodents like rats and mice constantly gnaw on objects to keep their teeth trimmed. Finding gnaw marks on furniture, walls, wires, or food packaging is a strong sign of a rodent infestation. Gnawing on electrical wires can even create a fire hazard. Rodent damage can be significant and costly to repair if left unaddressed. We can help identify and eliminate rodent entry points and nesting areas.

Unusual Odors

Some pests leave behind distinctive odors. For instance, rodents can leave a musky smell, while cockroaches can produce an oily or musty odor. Detecting unexplained or unpleasant odors in certain areas of your home could indicate a hidden pest infestation. These odors can be particularly strong in areas where pests are nesting or breeding. Relying on your sense of smell can be a helpful tool in pest detection.

Nesting Materials

Pests like rodents and birds often build nests in attics, walls, or crawl spaces. Finding shredded paper, fabric, or other debris in these areas can indicate the presence of a pest nest. Nests can harbor diseases and parasites. Locating and removing pest nests is an important part of comprehensive pest control. Our technicians are experienced in identifying and safely removing these nests.

Strange Noises

Hearing scratching, scurrying, or chirping noises within your walls, attic, or crawl space, especially at night, can be a sign of rodent or other pest activity. These sounds indicate that pests are moving around within your home’s structure. Identifying the source of these noises can help pinpoint the location of the infestation. Don’t ignore strange sounds; they are often a cry for help from your home.
